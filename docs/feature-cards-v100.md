# Capability cards v100

The feature section keeps the original three-card idea: sale, stock, report.
It replaces the v99 full-screen checkout and three separate screenshot proofs
with a more compact illustrated demonstration. A real checkout recording stays
available through the quiet “В программе · 14 с” link.

## Presentation

- Original “Продажи. Склад. Контроль.” heading.
- Three equally sized cards on desktop; native swipeable cards on phones.
- Phone selectors identify sale, stock and report. The next card peeks into view.
  Selecting or swiping a card does not advance the document vertically.
- Copy comes first, followed by a transparent product illustration and one
  compact outcome. Real program screens remain available as explicit links.
- Generated scanner, inventory cartons and chart objects share cobalt, pearl,
  chrome, lighting and perspective. Artwork has alpha transparency, two delivery
  sizes, no rasterized interface text and no image-shaped background.
- Rental and AI invoice links remain in a quiet supporting row.

## Data and motion

All values illustrate the existing recorded demo: 82,300 ₸ sale, two drills sold,
stock 12→10 and revenue 396,950→479,250 ₸. They are not live customer statistics.
Generated artwork is illustrative; preview links and video show the program UI.

Short value transitions start when the scene is visible. The settled result
remains readable for more than three seconds. Transitions repeat with a gentle
crossfade; cards never advance automatically. No replay or pause controls are
added. Motion pauses offscreen, while a dialog is open, or when the page is
hidden. System reduced motion displays static final values. Missing decoration
never removes the explanation, result or real interface link.

No changes to hero optics, other product sections, services, hosting or payment.
