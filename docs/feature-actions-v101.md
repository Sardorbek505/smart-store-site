# The product connects sale, stock and report

The three capability cards now demonstrate one action rather than showcasing
hardware. Repeated category labels and step numbers inside cards are removed.
The result belongs directly to each card: no nested white panel or extra border.

The scanner and receipt roll are replaced by a small, physically recognizable
drill cutout generated from the drill in the isolated demo catalog. The same
product appears in the sale and stock cards. The image has true alpha, two sizes,
and no baked-in text. It illustrates the demo item rather than a supported
hardware model or a product offered for sale by Smart Store.

- A drill row enters the basket before the total changes from 2,500 to 82,300 ₸.
  The 2,500 is a disc (650) and bit (1,850); two drills cost 79,800. Payment is
  confirmed after the basket total settles.
- The same two drills are deducted from stock: 12→10.
- The report shows +82,300 for that sale and revenue 396,950→479,250 ₸.

All values are demo data, not a live transaction. Short scenes retain a quiet
result hold, pause offscreen/in dialogs/on hidden pages, and show complete
static results for reduced motion and without JavaScript. Cards do not advance
automatically. Native phone swiping, keyboard navigation, real screenshot
previews and the optional recorded UI video remain available.

Other sections, hero effects, hosting and backends are unchanged.
